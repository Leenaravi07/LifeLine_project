Emergency Safety Tips

  • Stay calm and call emergency services when needed.
  • Tell the operator your location and what happened.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • Do not give food, drink, or medicine to an unconscious person.
  • Do not move someone with a suspected serious neck or spine injury unless there is immediate danger.
  • If someone is unconscious and not breathing normally, call emergency services and follow the operator's CPR instructions.
Important: LifeLine is a college project and is not an emergency dispatch service. This page does not send your location or alert emergency responders automatically. For a life-threatening emergency, call 112 directly.
